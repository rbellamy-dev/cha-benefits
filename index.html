The ledger

What one cup quietly does

Five effects, in order of evidence. No miracle claims. Just what decades of research keep finding, cup after cup.

01

Calm focusBest studied

L-theanine is a calming amino acid found only in tea. It rides alongside caffeine and softens its edges. The result is calm, steady focus that lasts for hours.

02

Cell defence

EGCG and its catechin family are antioxidants packed into green tea. They protect your cells from the wear of everyday life.

03

A steadier heart

Regular drinkers show lower LDL and better blood vessel function. This is the strongest signal in the research.

04

A faster idle

Catechins nudge the resting metabolic rate upward, helping the body prefer fat as fuel.

05

Clearer skin

Polyphenols give tea its bitterness and many of its benefits. They calm the mild inflammation that dulls skin. The change takes weeks, not mornings.

The craft

Don't guess. Steep.

Pick a leaf. The timer knows the temperature, the minutes, and the moment to stop. Watch the cup deepen as it works.

i.

Cool the water

Boil, then wait. This leaf wants 80°C. Boiling water scalds it and makes it bitter.

ii.

Measure the leaf

2g (one teaspoon) per 200ml. Use more leaf for a shorter steep. Never the reverse.

iii.

Let the timer finish

Steep for 2:00, lift the leaves, drink. The same leaves will give you two more infusions, each quieter than the last.
