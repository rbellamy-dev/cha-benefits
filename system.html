01

Paper and ink, lit by one green and one gold

Paper and ink carry the page. Green marks what matters on paper; gold does the same on the band. Never both at once.

Paper

  • --canvasThe page you're reading. Every light section; never pure white.
  • --canvas-cardLift state for hovered rows. A whisper of elevation.

Ink

  • --inkBody text and headings on paper.
  • --ink-softSupporting text: descriptions, captions.

Accent

  • --accentLinks, CTAs, active states, focus. Spent like ink.
  • --accent-deepHover and pressed fills; live timer numerals.

Lines

  • --hairlineInk at low opacity. The only border the system draws.

Band

  • --bandNight Pine. Hero, ritual, signup and footer.
  • --band-midThe lighter end of a band gradient.
  • --band-deepThe footer, and text on gold.

Gold and text on dark

  • --goldThe accent on the band only. Never on paper.
  • --on-darkWarm Paper reused as text on Night Pine.
  • --on-dark-softSecondary text on Night Pine, at 70 percent.

03

Drifts, never bounces

Two curves carry every transition. Both leave quickly and arrive slowly. With reduced motion turned on, each demo rests in its finished state.

Ease out

--ease-outUse for anything the reader causes: reveals, button lift, the tea picker.

Ease soft

--ease-softUse for slow ambient change: the cup filling, steam.

Blob frame

.blob-frameUse for every photograph. The one non-geometric shape in the system; drifts over 18 seconds.

05

Four rules, kept quietly

  1. 01

    One accent, used like ink: green on paper, gold on the band, never both.

    Do
    Spend it on links, active states and CTAs.
    Don't
    Use it as a large fill.
  2. 02

    One italic word per headline, on the word that carries the weight.

    Do
    Pick the word with the emotional payload of the line.
    Don't
    Italicise for convenience, or italicise two words.
  3. 03

    Motion drifts on a slow exponential curve.

    Do
    Give every animation a complete static version for reduced motion.
    Don't
    Reach for spring, bounce or elastic curves.
  4. 04

    No pure white, no pure black, no shadow on anything that isn't floating.

    Do
    Keep shadow for photographs, the cup, a hovered button.
    Don't
    Shadow a static card or container.
